### General Assessment

**1. Working Environment**  
The learning and internship environment at the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program is very open, dynamic, and encourages students to take initiative in research. Team members are always willing to discuss ideas, share resources, and provide timely technical troubleshooting support during hands-on labs and project implementation. Adding a few more networking or interactive sessions among internship groups would make team bonding even stronger.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor and team admins provided dedicated guidance and clear direction from the early stages of learning foundational AWS services to finalizing the capstone topic and system architecture. Rather than giving direct answers right away, they consistently asked thought-provoking questions so I could analyze the pros and cons of each option, which helped me understand the underlying technical principles deeply. The team admins were also very supportive with reporting workflows, templates, and answering inquiries promptly throughout the internship.

**3. Alignment Between Assigned Tasks and Academic Major**  
As an Artificial Intelligence major, I found the internship content highly complementary to my university studies. Building the **Driver Drowsiness & Distraction Detection** project allowed me to combine the Computer Vision knowledge (OpenCV, MediaPipe) I learned at school with AWS Serverless cloud infrastructure to deliver an end-to-end system capable of real-world authentication, storage, and monitoring.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**  
Throughout the internship, I not only gained hands-on proficiency in deploying and integrating core AWS services (IAM, API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, Cognito, S3, CloudFront, CloudWatch, etc.) but also developed an architectural mindset focused on security and cost optimization. In addition, maintaining weekly Worklogs, managing report documentation via GitHub/Hugo, and presenting architectural designs significantly improved my personal discipline and technical writing skills.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The spirit of knowledge sharing and mutual support within the team is very positive. Everyone respects individual perspectives and provides constructive feedback during progress reviews and system testing, creating a comfortable atmosphere that helped me become more confident in collaborative work.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The program offers a flexible roadmap and schedule well-suited for students. Having access to well-structured documentation, practical hands-on labs, and thematic sharing sessions from the FCAJ team served as valuable support for my learning and professional growth.

### Additional Questions

* **What are you most satisfied with during your internship?**  
  What satisfies me most is designing and deploying a complete end-to-end data pipeline for the capstone project—from the Edge AI device to AWS Serverless infrastructure—while mastering how to enforce security (JWT, IAM, OAC), prevent data loss or duplication during network outages, and manage operational costs rather than stopping at a local model prototype.

* **What do you think the program/organization should improve for future interns?**  
  The program could include a dedicated wrap-up session covering common configuration pitfalls (such as IAM permissions, API Gateway CORS settings, or cost guardrails) right before the capstone project phase so future interns can save troubleshooting time and deploy with greater confidence.

* **If recommending to friends, would you advise them to intern here? Why?**  
  I would definitely recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program to my friends, especially students looking for practical exposure to AWS cloud computing. The program provides a clear roadmap from foundational concepts to real-world project deployment, attentive mentorship, and practical experience in building complete systems.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  Organizing cross-team mock demo sessions before the final submission week would give interns the opportunity to observe different architectural approaches and refine their technical presentation skills.

* **Would you like to continue engaging with this program in the future?**  
  I would love to stay connected with the AWS Study Group / FCAJ community to continue learning more advanced topics in Cloud Security and system architecture going forward.

* **Other feedback (open sharing):**  
  I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the First Cloud AI Journey admin team for their dedicated guidance, support, and for creating the best possible conditions for me to successfully complete this internship.
